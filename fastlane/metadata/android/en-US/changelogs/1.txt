StopScroll 0.0.1, the first release. It closes YouTube's Shorts player as soon as it opens and leaves the rest of YouTube working: search, subscriptions and normal videos. Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, LinkedIn and X are listed as coming soon. No internet permission and no other permission, no ads, no account.
